How It Works

1. Report

Snap a photo of the pothole or damaged road. Our system automatically captures the exact GPS coordinates.

2. Route

The complaint is intelligently routed to the exact PWD/NHAI engineer responsible for that specific road stretch.

3. Resolve

Track the status live. The contractor is mandated to upload a verification photo once the issue is fixed.
